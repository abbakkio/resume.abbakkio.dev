# Statement of Intent: AI & Data Science Roadmap

## Overview
A dedicated `/roadmap` page for `cv.abbakkio.dev` designed in the exact aesthetic of the main portfolio (dark/light theme, `#ff5c00` accent, grid background, floating navigation dock). The page maps a 3-year, 9-trimester trajectory (2025–2028) that synchronizes university Mathematical & Computational Science (MCS) STEM courses with a parallel self-study track from roadmap.sh's AI and Data Scientist curriculum.

## User & Audience
- **Primary**: Azat Madiyev (operational study guide, milestone tracker, and semester-by-semester pacing).
- **Secondary**: Tech recruiters, engineering leads, and peers (visual proof of technical rigor and intentional specialization).

## Why Now
Currently in Year 1, Trimester 1 of the MCS Bachelor's program (2025–2028). Planning from day one prevents theoretical-only academic isolation and ensures industry-standard AI tooling develops alongside university math foundations.

## Core Pillars & Differentiators
1. **Mathematical & Computational Science Core**:
   - Calculus, Linear Algebra, Discrete Math, ODEs, PDEs, Computational Linear Algebra, Stochastic Processes, Numerical Methods, and High-Performance Computing.
2. **Synchronized AI & Data Science Self-Study (roadmap.sh)**:
   - Python data stack (NumPy, Pandas, Polars, SQL), Classical ML (Scikit-Learn, XGBoost), Deep Learning (PyTorch, Transformers), GenAI/LLM Engineering (RAG, Vector DBs, Agents), and MLOps (FastAPI, Docker, MLflow, CI/CD).
3. **Quantum Physics & Computing Specialization**:
   - Classical and Fluid Mechanics, Quantum Mechanics, Introduction to Quantum Computing, and Quantum Programming.
   - Bridges to Quantum Machine Learning (PennyLane, Qiskit, QNNs) and Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs).

## Status Tracking
- **Term 1 (Year 1, Trimester 1)**: Marked as `Active / In Progress`.
- **Terms 2–9**: Marked as `Upcoming / Planned Milestones`.

## Scope Boundaries
- **In Scope**:
  - Technical university courses from MCS 6B06107 curriculum.
  - Parallel self-study topics and milestone projects per term.
  - Client-side routing supporting `/roadmap` and hash navigation (`#roadmap`).
  - Seamless navigation back to the main portfolio and integration with the floating dock.
  - Type-safe, version-controlled static data model (`src/data/roadmap.ts`).
- **Out of Scope**:
  - Humanities / General Education university subjects (Russian/Kazakh languages, History of Kazakhstan, Philosophy, Physical Education, Cultural Studies, Sociology, Psychology, Political Science).
  - External database/backend tracking (maintained statically in code).
  - Modification of the existing home page resume cards.
